[Free download on the App Store / App Store 免費下載](https://apps.apple.com/app/apple-store/id6804534300?pt=129350645&ct=support_try_150&mt=8)

## Five minutes, no private files / 不用私人檔案也能試

1. Open the built-in offline demo. A fresh installation without external sources opens it automatically; it is also available from the home screen. No media-server account or network is needed for this demo.
2. Play the supplied short video, pause it, and try the supplied subtitles. Return to the demo library.
3. Open the sample photo and PDF, then return. Check whether the text and controls are easy to read on your device.
4. On iPhone or iPad, explore the sample text/subtitle or image-copy editor if available in your installed version. Cancel changes first. When saving a copy, choose a new disposable filename; never overwrite an important original for testing. Apple TV focuses on browsing and playback, not these editors.
5. Tell us one thing that worked and one thing that was confusing. A simple “I could not find…” is useful; you do not have to diagnose the bug.

1. 開啟內建離線示範。全新安裝、尚未設定外部來源時會自動進入，也可從首頁找到。示範不需要媒體伺服器帳號或網路。
2. 播放內附短片、暫停，試看內附字幕，再回到示範清單。
3. 開啟範例照片與 PDF，再返回，看看文字及按鈕是否清楚。
4. iPhone／iPad 可試用目前安裝版本提供的文字、字幕或圖片副本編輯。先試取消變更；儲存副本時請另取可丟棄的檔名，不要用重要原檔測試覆寫。Apple TV 以瀏覽播放為主，不提供上述編輯器。
5. 告訴我們一件順利的事和一件卡住的事。「找不到某個按鈕」也很有用，不需要自己找出程式原因。

## Optional: your own source / 可選：測試自己的來源

Use only an account/server you own or are authorized to access. Try one small, non-sensitive file first. Supported connection types include SMB, pCloud, WebDAV, Jellyfin, Files and direct HTTPS links. A provider's sharing webpage is not necessarily a direct media link. Do not send us your login, server address, token-bearing URL, or private files.

只使用自己的或獲授權的帳號／伺服器，先挑一個小型非敏感檔案。來源類型包括 SMB、pCloud、WebDAV、Jellyfin、「檔案」與 HTTPS 直連。雲端分享網頁不一定是可播放的直連；不需將登入資料、伺服器位址、帶 Token 的網址或私人檔案提供給我們。
